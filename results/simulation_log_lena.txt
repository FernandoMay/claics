PART V: Distributed Source Coding
       (Slepian-Wolf Coding)
========================================

--- Slepian-Wolf Theorem ---
For two correlated sources X and Y:
  R_X >= H(X|Y)
  R_Y >= H(Y|X)
  R_X + R_Y >= H(X,Y)
Separate encoding, joint decoding.

--- Hamming Distance ---
d_H(x,y) = sum_i (x_i XOR y_i)
Measures correlation between two binary sources.
